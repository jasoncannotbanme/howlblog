It's been a while since i touched this website, and for a reason. I haven't exactly been enjoying my life.
To be very fair im not depressed, nor am i looking for validation but deep inside, I know something aint right.

Technology has come far, way far than anyone expected and I wanted to do a lot with it but i can't, I cannot get *that right idea*. The idea that made zuckerberg sacrifice everything for even more. The idea that made george hotz who he is.

I guess i lack knowledge, Maybe i lack effort, or just maybe I dont wanna do anything because i might fail.

I know so much about so much, got so many ideas but someone already beat me to it because either i didnt work on it or because i wasn't original.

I've been working on something which i thought i could complete and ship to production easily within a week but i couldn't because everyday there's some new bullshit and i end up playing games or doing something else to numb myself out and let it fall for the next day as i take my sleep in regret *Nice, i didnt do shit*. 

Life's been distasteful because not only am i not doing anything or even if i am, My goals are too stupid and i have no patience to work it out and get myself to that dream place i always wanted, which is rich enough so i dont have to work ever again. Sounds fucking stupid right?

You know what, That is still fine, what gets me is that my efforts are minimal, i work on my project every 3 days, I cant explain it to anyone because i fear judgement and even if i do end up completing it, It wouldnt even take me close to 2% of where i wanna be 5 years from now.

It is fucking exhausing being average.

The only thing ive been consistent with is gym, Which i go everyday. What i haven't been consistent with is my body, It's not exactly making me jacked or healthy. I feel like the same i was when i started a year back. Why is it so hard to lose weight and reach a peak physique.

I wanna achieve so many things, and i wish i could blame it on someone else but i cant; It is always gonna be me whos holding myself back.
And **I dont know what to do**

Should i just take this time to learn everything or start building now. 
or should i trust the process and do what feels natural.

*Natural sounds boring*

I wanna do so many things but i dont know where to start. I've done everything but i keep entering the same cycle over and over again. 

Today's 4th august 2026; 5:17 IST. I'm working on entropy right now, I hope whenever i read it again, I have something that i can be proud about.

Cest la vie.
