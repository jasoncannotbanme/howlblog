This being the most talked about topic in AI right now, I thought I'd give my 2 cents.

I was fucking around reversing badlion client just trying stuff out because i kinda wanted to steal how [they optimise their FPS](https://www.badlion.net/optifine-and-betterframes-boost-your-minecraft-fps), more on that in another entropy focused blog.

What i realised was much weirder. Every decent AI model is capable of cracking literal suite of softwares, using MCP tools and shit like [REA](https://github.com/morluto/rea) so what if we use AI against AI? 

This term is commonly coined as the **AI Arm's Race** where every AI model is competing with each other to build and break encryptions at the same time. Major companies and their grandmas have adopted using AI to build encryption towards shit like fully decompilation and reconstruction of their software which they were using to milk the money out of every living human being. This has a good and bad side.

Let's start with the good side, You see software's getting cheap/replaced/fully free which you once thought was pretty much not gonna happen at all, now thanks to MCP tools and brainless prompts you now have photoshop reconstructed in a 1:1 port. Amazing right?

The bad side being, Every conglomerate is gonna start targetting AI models to make them more expensive or straight up make it a privilage for people to use and you are gonna see it happen. For example claude mythos, Goverment's acted like it was a fucking war and put the model down for good by telling claude to take it off unless they got a taste first. I may or may not be changing words out pure hate but believe me shit like this is gonna happen much more than you think

The second part to this bad side, we are gonna be fed slower or less dense AI models which will end up making us stop using it out of less performance or make us give up, but there are solutions for this, the only problem is, its gonna be sooner than we think and we havent reached the computational greatness (financially speaking) to get the parts to make a smart local AI model for cheap. I mean I genuinely see a price hike coming out of nowhere when NVIDIA's factories report an okay rate of production, but thats just a consipracy... right?

I mean economist's call it demand & supply chain but if we know anything better, Everything's fucking masked into a term, or an excuse to skim money out of people, because the data shows no matter how expensive an iphone is, Varun Jaat from gurgoan is gonna preorder it. 

Alright, now the third part which is tending more towards how software and hardware are gonna be built in the future. Say there is no intervention between how AI is handling cracking and building obscure encryptions to safeguard software's under command and destroying those said encryptions. All of this is gonna create the glory hole of vibe coding slop. 

I'm not claming companies dont manually review code but how often does it happen, my own business partner has claude & antigravity running on `--dangerously-skip-permissions` 24/7. That shit alone makes me think that nothing original is gonna be left in the next 5-10 years but this claim is far fetched, I'd still like to think its somewhat true, And not to mention this claim goes not only towards software and hardware, It also goes towards content, traffic on the internet, art, music etc as we have been seeing the past few months/years.

Who will win this arms race you might ask? well we already have a winner and they are the people handling the AI models and the people who invested long before this shit got famous

what can you do? nothing really.
